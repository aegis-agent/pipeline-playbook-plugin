# /growth - Parasitic Twitter Growth

30-day strategy by engaging with larger accounts.

## Philosophy

> "Find the 10 biggest accounts in your niche. Be FIRST to reply with something actually valuable."

## Usage

```
/growth "<niche>" [--accounts <list>] [--days <N>]
```

### Examples

```bash
/growth "saas founders"
/growth "ai" --accounts "@sama,@kaborashid"
/growth "devtools" --days 14
```

## Reply Framework

**What NOT to do:** "Great post!", "This fire", "Agree!"

**What TO do:**
- Add specific insight or data
- Ask thoughtful follow-up question
- Share relevant personal experience
- Offer contrarian (but respectful) take
